    Didn't vote

    Voting is optional at 70+ and 16–17. 80+ is shown apart: the 2026 roll lists 297,977 voters aged 100 or older, and 99% of that group didn't vote in 2022, so the band includes people no longer living. Leaving 80+ out takes them out of the dots and rates. Ages for 2026 are estimated.

    Filter places

    Ranges move by number of municipalities. Totals add up the municipalities that pass; rates in small places swing widely, and a place's average doesn't describe each voter.
